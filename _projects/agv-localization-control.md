A mobile-robot project focused on localization and trajectory tracking.

- Implemented an **Extended Kalman Filter (EKF)** using encoder, IMU, and LiDAR measurements for state estimation and localization.
- Developed a **PID-based trajectory-tracking controller** for references generated from joystick commands.
- Integrated **YOLOv8-based perception** for human detection.
