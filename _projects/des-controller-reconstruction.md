This work was carried out during my final-year Bachelor research internship at **LURPA, ENS Paris-Saclay**.

- Investigated controller reconstruction in **Discrete Event Systems (DES)** through system identification.
- Derived conditions under which an identified model can be used for **exact controller reconstruction**.
- Developed an algorithm for **automatic controller synthesis from observed data**.

The project formed the basis of my Bachelor's thesis, graded **18.7/20**.
